Gunsmithing

Expert work on your rifle from gunsmiths who run the same suppressors they build. No shortcuts, no guesswork — just tight work done right.

What We Do

Services

From barrel threading for your new suppressor to full trigger system overhauls. Serving competitive shooters, law enforcement, and serious enthusiasts.

Barrel Threading

Precision threading to 1/2×28 or 5/8×24 (and others on request) to accept suppressor mounts. We crown and verify concentricity before threading. Essential before mounting any Hickory Hill suppressor.

Starting at $95 · 3–5 day turnaround

Trigger Work

Drop-in trigger installations, polishing existing FCGs, and full trigger system diagnostics. We work with Geissele, LaRue, CMC, and other quality drop-ins, as well as tuning factory triggers to spec.

Starting at $120 · 2–3 day turnaround

NFA Compliance

SBR conversions, engraving for trust/entity transfers, and SOT paperwork assistance. We'll walk you through the full Form 1 or Form 4 process. Everything done by the book, every time.

Starting at $75 · Call for timeline

Diagnostics & Repair

Reliability issues, timing problems, bolt carrier work, and feeding/ejection troubleshooting. We run your rifle through a full function check and range test before returning it.

$75 diagnostic fee · Parts quoted separately

Suppressor Mount Fitting

Proper HUB and direct-thread mount installation, concentric bore alignment, and baffle strike detection. We verify every mount is square before it leaves our bench.

Starting at $55 · Same day available

Custom Builds

Full suppressor-optimized AR builds from stripped lowers up. Gas system tuning, adjustable BCG selection, and buffer/spring optimization for suppressed use. We spec the whole system.

Call for quote · Typical 2–4 weeks
