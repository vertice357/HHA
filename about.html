About Us

Our Story

Hickory Hill Arms started with one idea: build the suppressor we couldn't find anywhere else. One that could survive what we put it through. That still looked incredible after 10,000 rounds.

Who We Are

Shooters who build
for shooters.

Hickory Hill Arms was founded on a simple premise: the suppressor market was full of acceptable. We wanted exceptional. Products that a competitive shooter, a law enforcement officer, and a serious enthusiast could all trust on day one and on day ten thousand.

Every Hickory Hill suppressor begins as a billet of 17-4 stainless steel. Not a casting. Not a sintered tube. A solid billet, machined to tight tolerances, then heat-treated to H900 at 1600°F — the same process used in aerospace fasteners and marine hardware.

We subject every design to BOCOG stress testing and verify MIL-STD-1474D fullband sound performance before it enters our catalog. The Deadridge and Nightfall aren't just products — they're the culmination of years of iteration with the people who actually run these tools in the field.

As a licensed FFL/SOT, we handle Form 4 NFA transfers in-house. We believe buying a suppressor should be straightforward, and we're here to make that happen — from order placement to transfer completion.

How We Build

Our Process

Every suppressor goes through the same eight-step process. No shortcuts at any stage.

01
Material Selection
17-4 PH stainless steel billet, certified to AMS 5643 and ASTM A564. Full material traceability on every batch.
02
CNC Machining
5-axis CNC machining to ±0.0005" tolerances. Baffles, endcaps, and tubes all machined in-house — no outsourced components.
03
Heat Treatment
H900 condition heat treat at 1600°F minimum. Achieves 190,000 PSI tensile strength — the hardest condition available in 17-4.
04
Bore Alignment Check
Every tube is verified for bore concentricity before assembly. Eliminates baffle strike risk from the start.
05
Assembly
Welded and staked per spec. No adhesive-only joints. Each assembly torqued to documented specification and marked.
06
Stress Testing
BOCOG-protocol stress test on representative samples from every production run. 60,000 rounds. Full-auto. No failures allowed.
07
Sound Performance Verification
Measured against MIL-STD-1474D fullband standard. We publish actual dB figures — not marketing specs.
08
Final Inspection & Serialization
100% visual and dimensional inspection. Serialized, engraved per ATF requirements, and packaged for transfer.
NFA Guide

How the Transfer Works

Suppressors are NFA-regulated items requiring ATF approval. Here's exactly how the process works when you buy from us.

1
Order & Payment
Place your order online. We take full payment at checkout. This locks in your price and secures your serialized unit.
2
Choose Transfer Method
Transfer to HHA directly (if we're your SOT) or we ship Form 3 to your local SOT. Either way, we guide you through the paperwork.
3
Complete ATF Form 4
Individual, trust, or corporation — we help you fill out the Form 4. Submit with your $200 tax stamp payment to the ATF.
4
Wait for Approval
Current ATF processing times are approximately 4–8 months for individual Form 4s. We'll notify you the moment your stamp is approved.
5
Take Possession
Once your Form 4 is approved, pick up your suppressor from us or your local SOT. Keep a copy of your approved Form 4 with the can at all times.

Common Questions

Do I need a trust?
No. Individuals can purchase suppressors without a trust since the ATF's 41F rule change in 2016. A trust still offers benefits (multiple trustees can possess the item) but is no longer required.
How long does approval take?
Current average is 4–8 months for individual Form 4 transfers. This varies by ATF workload. We'll give you real-time status updates throughout.
What's the $200 tax stamp?
The NFA requires a one-time $200 transfer tax on each NFA item (suppressor, SBR, SBS, MG). This is paid directly to the ATF when you file your Form 4. It is not included in our product price.
Can I shoot it while waiting for approval?
No. You cannot take possession of or use the suppressor until your Form 4 is fully approved by the ATF. The item stays with the dealer until then.
What states allow suppressors?
As of 2024, 42 states allow civilian suppressor ownership. The prohibited states are: CA, DE, HI, IL, MA, NJ, NY, RI. Always confirm your state's current laws before purchasing.
